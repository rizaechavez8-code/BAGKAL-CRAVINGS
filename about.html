About Us

Anti-gutom cravings Davao is a school project platform connecting home-based food sellers with customers. Sellers can post their menus, set availability, and receive pre-orders. Customers can browse, search, and reserve meals ahead of time.

Our mission is to support local home cooks and make ordering simple, fast, and reliable for the Davao community.
